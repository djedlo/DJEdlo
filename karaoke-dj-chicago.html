Karaoke DJ FAQ

How much does a karaoke DJ cost in Chicago?
DJ Edlo's karaoke DJ services in Chicago start at $600 for private home karaoke parties, $750 for bar and venue karaoke nights, and $800 for corporate karaoke events. All packages include professional sound equipment, wireless microphones, a large display screen, and the full 50,000+ song library. Contact us for a custom quote for your specific event.
Does DJ Edlo have Spanish karaoke songs?
Yes! DJ Edlo's library includes 5,000+ Spanish-language karaoke songs covering Latin pop, reggaeton, salsa, bachata, cumbia, banda, norteño and more. He also hosts in both English and Spanish making him the perfect karaoke DJ for multicultural events and Latin parties in Chicago.
Can DJ Edlo do karaoke AND DJ at the same event?
Absolutely! This is one of DJ Edlo's most popular packages. He combines karaoke rounds with DJ sets so your event never loses energy. Karaoke can open the night and transition into a dance party, or alternate throughout the evening. It's perfect for birthdays, corporate events, and any party where you want both activities.
Does DJ Edlo bring all the karaoke equipment?
Yes — DJ Edlo brings everything: professional PA speakers, 2 wireless microphones, a large display monitor for lyrics, a tablet-based song request system so guests can browse and request from their phones, and all cables, stands and backup equipment. You just need a power outlet and a place to set up.
What types of karaoke events does DJ Edlo host in Chicago?
DJ Edlo hosts all types of karaoke events in Chicago: bar and venue karaoke nights, private home karaoke parties, birthday party karaoke, bachelorette party karaoke, corporate team building karaoke, holiday party karaoke, anniversary parties, and recurring bar karaoke nights. No event is too small or too large.
How far in advance should I book a karaoke DJ in Chicago?
For private karaoke parties we recommend booking 4-8 weeks in advance. For bar and venue karaoke nights, 2-4 weeks is usually sufficient. Friday and Saturday nights fill up fastest — especially during summer and holiday season — so book early to lock in your preferred date.
