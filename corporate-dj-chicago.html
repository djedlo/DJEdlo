Corporate DJ FAQ

How much does a corporate DJ cost in Chicago?
DJ Edlo's corporate DJ services start at $800 for the Essential package (holiday parties and team events), $1,000 for the Premium package (galas and award ceremonies), and $1,500 for the Corporate Complete package which includes DJ, professional MC services, and a photo booth with custom branded strips. All packages include a free planning consultation. Contact us for a custom quote based on your event size, duration, and specific needs.
Does DJ Edlo offer a free consultation for corporate events?
Yes — a free planning consultation is included with every corporate DJ booking. DJ Edlo will meet with you by phone or Zoom to discuss your event timeline, program structure, music preferences, must-play and do-not-play lists, venue details, dress code expectations, and any special announcements or award presentations. This ensures your corporate event runs perfectly from setup to final song.
What types of corporate events does DJ Edlo DJ in Chicago?
DJ Edlo provides professional entertainment for all types of corporate events in Chicago including company holiday parties, corporate galas, award ceremonies, product launches, fundraisers, charity events, team building events, office parties, client appreciation events, annual company celebrations, and networking events. No corporate event is too large or too small.
Does DJ Edlo provide MC services for corporate events?
Yes — professional MC services are included in all corporate packages. DJ Edlo handles all program announcements, award presentations, executive introductions, crowd engagement, and program transitions in a polished, professional manner appropriate for corporate settings. He is also fully bilingual in English and Spanish for multicultural corporate events and diverse company teams.
Can DJ Edlo add a photo booth to our corporate event?
Absolutely! The Corporate Complete package includes a professional photo booth with unlimited photos, instant prints, custom branded photo strips featuring your company logo, fun props, and a digital gallery that all guests can access after the event. It's consistently one of the most popular additions to holiday parties, galas and team events. The photo booth can also be added to any package as an upgrade.
What Chicago venues does DJ Edlo perform corporate events at?
DJ Edlo has performed corporate events at venues throughout Chicago and Chicagoland including hotels, ballrooms, rooftops, conference centers, restaurants, private clubs and outdoor venues in the Loop, River North, West Loop, Gold Coast, Rosemont, Oak Brook, Naperville, Schaumburg, Lisle, Northbrook and more.
